# Clicking "answered" or "modified" when answers are paginated should go to the answer anyway

A high-traffic question (The second draft of our Code of Conduct is available for feedback and review ) showed up at the top of the questions list today with a new answer from a low-rep user.

Because I knew that question had a lot of answers, I clicked on the word answered to go straight to the answer. Instead I just found myself at the top of the question page. This is what happens on sites where I have a lower rep and can't see deleted posts; if I click answered for a deleted answer I just see the question. But I can see deleted posts on meta.

I found the answer eventually towards the bottom of page 2 of the 3 pages of answers. While questions whose answers need to paginate are a bit of an edge case, they are one of the times we need this feature the most. Please make it work.
