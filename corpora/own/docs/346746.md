# What is the proper capitalization of "Question" and "Answer"?

Basically, I've been capitalizing Question and Answer when referring to Stack Exchange Questions and Answers - that is, the pieces of content on the site - to distinguish them from just questions and answers in a more generic sense. For instance, "Please don't answer in the comments. Write your answers in the Answers." After someone asked me why I was doing that, though, I realized that there might be an official way of referring to them, and I figured that I could ask about it on here.

So, does Stack Exchange officially have any capitalization or style guidelines for referring to Questions and Answers on their site?
