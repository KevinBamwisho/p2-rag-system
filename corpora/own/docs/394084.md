# A couple of typos in the moderator questionnaire

There are some minor bugs in the 2023 Moderator Election Questionnaire:
(e.g. at https://music.stackexchange.com/election)

Question 1 has a grammar mistake:

How would you deal with a user who produced [produces] a steady stream of valuable answers, but tends to generate a large number of arguments/flags from comments?

Question 3 has a typo:

There is a new user that posts a few answers with the same link that may or may not be their own site or blog. Sometimes it seems like an appropriate resource, on other questions it 'snot [it's not] very relevant. One of the user's posts has been flagged as spam due to this. How do you approach handling this flag and the new user in general?

Also, meta-tags in answers (such as bug, status-review) are rendering in the colour scheme of regular tags.
