# Really old question draft appears in Safari after machine restart

After doing a lot of editing yesterday and today in the Math SE Ask a Question box on Safari, when I absentmindedly restarted my laptop (MacBook Pro) without thinking about the possible consequences, Safari put a really old draft in the textbox and all my recent editing appears to be lost forever.

This naturally leads me to wonder what good "Draft saved" is. I usually copy/paste into a Notepad document after every new sentence, but I fell out of the habit with this recent editing. Fortunately, I didn't lose that much, but it's hard to remember exactly what I wrote.
