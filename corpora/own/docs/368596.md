# Post a question to generate tag wikis

Over on Sustainability.SE we just issued a call for tag wikis. Historically, we've struggled to generate tag wikis, and those we do have aren't always great.

To address this problem (in addition to the meta post), I wondered about a different method to encourage people to contribute to tag wikis: Post a question on the main site asking for the definition and usage of [tag].

Use a title like "What is [tag], and how does it relate to [site topic]?"
A tag like tag-wiki-generator could be used to identify the questions
After a while, the top-voted answer could be edited into the tag wiki

I understand that this would be borderline off-topic for most sites. A question like "What is jQuery?" would get closed in a heartbeat on SO, and rightfully so. But on a smaller site and for more niche topics, such definition questions are often on topic. For instance, on Sustainability.SE we have a well-used tag for defining-sustainability.

This approach has a few benefits I can see:

The topic discussion would be much more visible than a post on per-site meta
People would be more likely to vote, and answers would earn rep for the posters, giving some motivation to contribute

Obviously, before implementing this we'd have to discuss on our per-site meta, but I wanted to propose the idea here to get some feedback from those active on other sites.

Some discussion questions:

Have other sites tried this? What were the results?
Any other benefits to this approach?
Any drawbacks I'm not seeing?
