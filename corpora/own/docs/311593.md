# Logging in to the Area 51 Discussion Zone will successfully log you into the main Area 51 site, but not the Discussion Zone itself

In the Area 51 Discussion Zone (DZ), the log in link is for area51.stackexchange.com, not area51.meta.stackexchange.com.

Because of domain restrictions, etc., after successfully logging in you get redirected back to the Discussion Zone, and although you are logged into the main Area 51 site, you aren't logged into the DZ. If you come to meta.stackexchange.com then you can log in and the single sign in will carry across to the DZ.
