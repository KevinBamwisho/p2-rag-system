# The bounty gambit: Is it misuse to add e.g. a 50-point bounty aiming to attract >5 upvotes?

On some of my posts (like this one) I feel like it would be worthwhile playing the bounty gambit: add a bounty with the sole aim of getting those points back and more in upvotes. E.g. a 50-point bounty gambit needs to attract >5 upvotes to succeed.

Question: Is it misuse to add e.g. a 50-point bounty aiming to attract >5 upvotes?

It's somewhat unscrupulous: I have no reason to do this other than potentially gaining more reputation than I lose, and perhaps it's a bit disruptive.
