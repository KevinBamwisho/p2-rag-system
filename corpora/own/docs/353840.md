# Why are tag wiki edits from low-rep users split into two requests?

Some time ago, I suggested an edit to the cypress tag wiki on Stack Overflow, as it was poorly worded and lacking in my view. I made the changes I saw fit, and submitted my suggestion.

To my surprise, my edit was split into two requests. One was approved, but the other was rejected. I stand by both edits, but I can understand why a reviewer might reject the second; I believe it improved the flow, and it fixed an awkward run-on, but I would not have submitted it alone and I can see why it may have seemed superfluous.

To this day, it stands as my only rejected edit.

I intended the edits to be proposed together, and that's what I thought would happen. I was following the guidance to make edits as complete as possible, and there was no indication that this was two edits, not just one:

Your edit (singular) will be placed in a queue until it is peer reviewed.

Here is what the tag edit page looks like for low-rep users:

And below the edit preview, here's the change description and submit button:

I may have missed it, but I see no indication that excerpt and wiki edits will be separated.

I'd like to request one of two things:

Bundle tag wiki edits as one edit request, or
Use explicit language to indicate that edits will be split.
