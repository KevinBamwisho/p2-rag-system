# What happens when a late answer is upvoted?

I have a doubt concerning the way the Late Answers review queue works. Suppose that, at the moment that an answer is posted, it qualifies to be sent to the Late Answers review queue. That is (please correct me if I am wrong), the poster's reputation is below or equal to 50 points and the question was posted more than 30 days ago. But then (before it was sent to the queue) the answer (or some other post posted by the same user) is upvoted and then the reputation of the person who posted it rises above 50 points. Will the answer still be sent to the Late Answers review queue?
