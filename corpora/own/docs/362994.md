# Can the Stack Exchange websites work with a small cookie notification?

The massive cookie acceptance notification is a major annoyance. Even more annoying, is that even if I've logged into Stack Overflow, when I visit another Stack Exchange website, I'm shown the annoying notification again and I don't even get automatically logged in. Incognito tabs.

Earlier when it was a thin notification at the bottom of the page which could be dismissed by one click, it wasn't all that annoying. But now it's thrown in our face and we have to click twice if we don't want cookies tracking us.

Please make that notification smaller. By default, nobody wants cookies tracking them. That's a given. If someone is ok with the tracking, you can give them one button that says "I consent".
