# Keyword-based spam detection to keep spam questions out of the network

Over the course of the Winter Bash, I have encountered a lot of spam questions here on MSE, the common pattern among them being the keyword "keto diet" or simply "keto".

No doubt they are deleted as soon as they are posted (thanks to the active community), but isn't there a method already in place which helps prevent such cases? If not, is there a plan to introduce/try one?

To begin with, one can check the user's history of questions to see if they have been asking questions with similar keywords earlier, and whether those questions were closed/deleted. This can then be expanded to consider posts made by that user across the SE network to prevent spam.

Thoughts?
