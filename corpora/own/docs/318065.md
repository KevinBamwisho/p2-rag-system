# "Candidates answers your questions" for older elections links to recently held elections' meta page

I checked with the election (site_address/election) page of Stack Overflow, Super User, and Workplace, and I noticed that on the page of any year's elections, Candidates answers your questions is always linked to the relevant page of the recently held elections.

So if you pick the 2013 election for Stack Overflow, the link to [the] candidates answer your questions links to the relevant page of 2018 elections. The same goes for all the older elections pages of the three sites I tested with.
