# Extending media from photos to small video and audio

Photos have a limit of 2 MB. Communication is greatly improved when a small (less than 2 MB) .mov or similar file could be posted to reproduce observations for mechanics.stackexchange.com and possibly other sites. For example, reproducing sounds often aid in the diagnosis of mechanical difficulties.

I would think it to be in the interest, if not strategic, of SE to retain custody of said assets and not cede them to YouTube or other site.

How would such a feature request to enable all media less than 2 MB be submitted?
