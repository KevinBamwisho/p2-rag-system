# In Stack Overflow en español, review audits do not show in the activity tab

On October 2018, review audits were activated in Stack Overflow en español. They have been working well and we all are very happy.

However, I noticed that audit reviews do not show in the activity page of users, while they are supposed to.

For example, I got a review on October 30:

https://es.stackoverflow.com/review/low-quality-posts/122646

But it does not show in the 'activity tab' on my profile:

(it should show between reviews on 26 and 30)

However, on November 19 I passed an audit on First Posts (Primera publicación) and it does show:

So, we now have a case of First Post showing but Low Quality not.

Initially reported on Meta SOes but asked to post it in Meta.SE also.
