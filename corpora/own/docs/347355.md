# Inconsistent rounding of "months ago"

Today I saw a question that was deleted by the "1 year without activity" roomba. Curiously, it said "asked 1 year, 3 months ago" and "deleted 4 months ago". Was it deleted too early, I wondered? No: turns out it was deleted more than 1 year after being asked. The "asked ... ago" time is being rounded down and the "deleted ... ago" is being rounded up.

Why is the rounding inconsistent here? Tagged very-minor-pedantic-bug :-)
